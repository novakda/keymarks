        Settings

        Click the box and press the keys, e.g. Ctrl+Alt+P. Backspace clears it. The tray icon's menu lists the top results of your current search.

        Backups

        A backup is taken every time the app starts (the newest 10 are kept), before keyword cleanups and restores, and whenever your collection reaches a new size ("Largest collection", kept even if you later delete a lot). Restoring saves your current bookmarks first.

        Clean up keywords

        Review what will be removed or merged; click a group to show or hide its keywords. Tagged keywords are never touched. A backup of the whole database is saved first.
